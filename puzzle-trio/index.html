Puzzle Trio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Developer: 문학소정

This policy applies to the Android games Orbit Garden (com.pocketgarden.orbit), Trio Critters (com.pocketgarden.trio), and Orchard Mosaic (com.pocketgarden.orchard).

Gameplay data

These games do not require an account. Progress and high scores are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay progress. Clear app storage or uninstall the game to remove it.

Advertising and data

The Android apps use Google AdMob banner ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect and process advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics, and device information for advertising, fraud prevention, and operation. Google and its advertising partners process this data under their policies. See Google's privacy policy and AdMob data information.

Where required, the app asks for consent before requesting ads and displays an advertising privacy choices button when applicable. Android settings may offer advertising identifier controls.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell gameplay progress or operate an account database. AdMob data may be shared with Google and its advertising partners according to Google's terms and your choices. Google's retention and deletion controls apply to data it processes. Local progress can be deleted through app storage settings or uninstalling.

Children

These are general audience puzzle games and are not specifically directed to children. Advertising behavior may differ by region and consent choice.

Updates and contact

We will update this page when app features or data practices change. For questions or privacy requests, contact 5516psj@gmail.com.
