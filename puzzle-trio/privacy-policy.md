# Privacy Policy | Orbit Garden, Trio Critters, Orchard Mosaic

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Developer: 문학소정
Contact: 5516psj@gmail.com

This policy applies to the Android games Orbit Garden (com.pocketgarden.orbit), Trio Critters (com.pocketgarden.trio), and Orchard Mosaic (com.pocketgarden.orchard).

## Gameplay data

These games do not require an account. Progress and high scores are stored on the device. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay progress. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game removes locally stored progress.

## Advertising and data

The Android apps use Google AdMob to show banner ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process the device advertising identifier, app activity such as ad views and interactions, approximate location inferred from network information, diagnostics, and device information for advertising, fraud prevention, and service operation. Google processes this information under its own policies. Read [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's advertising data information](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

Where required, the app asks for consent before requesting ads and provides an advertising privacy choices control when the consent platform requires it. Device settings may also provide advertising ID controls.

## Sharing and retention

We do not sell gameplay progress or operate an account database. AdMob data may be shared with Google and its advertising partners in accordance with Google's terms and the choices presented to you. Google's retention and deletion controls apply to data it processes. For locally stored progress, clear app storage or uninstall the app.

## Children

The games are general audience puzzle games and are not specifically directed to children. Advertising behavior may vary by region and consent choice.

## Updates and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The updated date appears above. For questions or privacy requests, email 5516psj@gmail.com.
